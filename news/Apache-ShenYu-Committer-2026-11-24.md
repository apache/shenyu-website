### Personal Introduction

Hello everyone, I'm Jinze Song. You can find me on GitHub at [BobSong-dev](https://github.com/BobSong-dev).

My interests mainly lie in Java backend development, API gateways, and AI agent engineering, particularly in exploring how MCP capabilities can be integrated into gateway architectures.

I'm honored to become an Apache ShenYu Committer. This is an important milestone in my open-source journey and an opportunity to take on greater responsibilities within the community.

### Discovering Apache ShenYu

My journey with Apache ShenYu began through the Open Source Promotion Plan (OSPP). I started by exploring the codebase and understanding the project's architecture, gradually moving on to development work related to Agent Gateway.

As I became more involved, I gained a deeper understanding of gateway internals and the engineering challenges behind building a mature open-source project. I also came to appreciate that a successful project depends not only on continuous technical improvements, but also on communication, collaboration, and shared experience among its community members.

### Open Source Contributions and Growth

During my time contributing to Apache ShenYu, I primarily focused on Agent Gateway development. My work included the design and implementation of request-scoped context capabilities, as well as exploring and developing MCP-related features.

These contributions gave me the opportunity to better understand gateway request processing, context management, and the engineering challenges of integrating AI capabilities into traditional gateway architectures.

Throughout the development process, I learned that implementing a feature is only one part of making a meaningful contribution. Understanding the existing architecture, considering different use cases, maintaining code quality, and clearly communicating design decisions during pull request discussions are equally important.

Through code reviews and technical discussions, I have gained valuable engineering experience and developed a deeper appreciation for open-source collaboration and high-quality software development.

### Acknowledgements

I would like to express my sincere gratitude to my mentor, Hongyu Liu (Aias00), for his guidance and support throughout my technical work and community involvement.

I am also thankful to everyone in the Apache ShenYu community. The thoughtful feedback during code reviews and the knowledge shared through technical discussions have helped me grow tremendously. I truly appreciate being part of such an open, welcoming, and collaborative community.

### Looking Ahead

Becoming an Apache ShenYu Committer marks a new chapter in my open-source journey.

I look forward to continuing my work on Agent Gateway and related capabilities, while taking a more active role in pull request reviews, issue tracking, and community discussions.

I also hope to share what I have learned through my open-source experience and help new contributors find their way into the community.

I am excited to continue this journey alongside the Apache ShenYu community and contribute to the project's growth.
